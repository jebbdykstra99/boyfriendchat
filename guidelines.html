Community guidelines for boyfriendchat

These guidelines sit alongside our Terms of Service. If they differ, the Terms control.

Not allowed

  • Intimate images of a person shared without their consent, including realistic AI-made or edited images.
  • Copyrighted material you don’t have the rights to post.
  • Violent, gory or offensive images.
  • Hateful or racist images.
  • Any sexual content involving a minor. We hide it immediately, preserve it as federal law requires, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • Illegal activity, scams, malware, impersonation, and other people’s private data.

Remove first

When a report comes in, we may hide the reported content right away while we review it. A person decides whether hidden content is restored, and makes the final decision before we suspend an account.

We review reports and act on them as quickly as we can. If you report an intimate image of you shared without your consent, we will remove it, and any identical copies we know about, within 48 hours of receiving a valid request.

How to report

Takedown contact: takedown@subx.it (intimate images, copyright, violent or offensive images, hateful images, and anything that may involve a minor).

Tap Report on a post or story and choose a reason, or see Report / takedown request. For copyright, see our copyright policy.
