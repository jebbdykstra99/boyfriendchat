Privacy (preview)

boyfriendchat is a peer-story preview on Firebase project subx-skins. His stories. Your table. Jebb will replace this text. Short version of what this shell does today:

  • Email/password auth (Google is off until that provider is enabled).
  • Account docs live at users/{uid} with a display name, email, and siteId.
  • Posts are public reads. Images go to Storage under posts/boyfriend/{uid}/.
  • Poll votes are stored on the post as poll.votes.{uid}. Guests cannot vote.
  • No third-party social ingest. No AI on the hot path. Peer stories — not a dating funnel, not therapy.

If you want an account removed, contact the operator of this preview. This stub is not a DPA, CCPA pack, or counsel opinion.
